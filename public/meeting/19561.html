What happened AI-written, checked

On October 22, 2026, the City Council passed two ordinances (city laws) allowing sewer revenue bonds of up to $100,000,000 each.[12][13][14][15]

The council passed a one-year, $1,000,000 cooperative agreement with the Mid America Assistance Coalition (Ordinance 260905).[24][25]

It also passed Ordinance 260906, which appropriates $2,000,000 for added funding to the Blue Hills Townhomes project.[26][27]

Ordinance 260918 moves $485,424 in marijuana sales tax money to the Extreme Weather Program, and the council passed it.[17][18]

Ordinance 260445, the Country Club Plaza sidewalk right-of-way vacation, was held until November 5th.[3]

Many other items were sent to committees, including Ordinance 260941, which would allow taxable industrial revenue bonds up to $572,500,000.[37][38][39][40]
